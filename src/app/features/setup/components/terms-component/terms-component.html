Connection & Data Use

Store connection and your data

By connecting your store, you let Miyya securely read the information it needs to work.

Store connection

You stay in control of your store.

  • The connection is approved automatically. Press Allow & connect and Miyya approves it for you, so you skip WooCommerce's own Approve screen. Pressing the button is your consent.
  • Automatic approval creates a read-only key in your store (WooCommerce → Settings → Advanced → REST API) and sends it securely to Miyya. You can revoke it there at any time.
  • If automatic approval isn't possible, you'll see WooCommerce's own Approve screen. Nothing connects until you approve it there.
  • Miyya only reads your products, categories, prices and stock. It can't add, change or delete anything in your store.
  • Disconnect any time from Settings. This stops all future access.

Your data stays private

We handle your store data with care.

  • Connecting authorizes sending your store details, catalog and chat context to Miyya for the hosted service. Read-only order access supports questions from signed-in customers.
  • Miyya sends product text and relevant chat context to OpenAI for embeddings and AI-generated answers. Do not include unnecessary personal or sensitive information in chat.
  • We protect your data with reasonable security measures.
